Privacy policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

AI Job Search is a private tool used only by its owner, Håvard Virik, on his own computer. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission (Google OAuth, the read-only gmail.readonly scope), it reads messages in the owner's own Gmail account: sender, subject, date and message text.

How the data is used

Only to find replies from employers about the owner's job applications and to suggest status updates to his personal application tracker, which he approves one by one. Emails are processed on the owner's computer; the tracker and the IDs of messages already checked are stored there.

What it does not do

It does not send, delete, label or change any email, does not access anyone else's account, does not sell or share data with anyone, and does not use it for advertising.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
